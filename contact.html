Frequently Asked Questions

What is TuneFry?

TuneFry is a music promotion marketplace that helps artists get real visibility with playlisting, content promotion, and expert guidance.

How does TuneFry promote music?

We use curated playlists, social media influencer collaborations, and targeted strategies based on your music genre and goals.

How can I join Tunefry?

Joining Tunefry is simple — you just need to create an account, complete your artist profile, and upload your music content to start sharing it with the world.

Is there a fee to use Tunefry?

Tunefry offers both free and premium plans; the premium plan unlocks extra distribution, analytics, and marketing support for professional growth.
